Overtime sessions

Midnight is handled automatically: for example, 16:00–05:00 ends the next morning. Afternoon-shift overtime after 00:00 is assigned to the next calendar day. Before and After Shift are calculated separately, each starting with the first-hour multiplier. Both payments are added to your total.

Pay settings

The IDR 33,121 hourly rate is independent of the IDR 5,730,100 salary. Changing the salary does not change the overtime rate.

Payroll deductions & PPh 21

Employee tax status: TK/0 — monthly TER Category A. Regular monthly withholding is an estimate; the final tax period uses annual reconciliation.

TER applies to taxable gross, before employee JHT/JPN deductions. JHT and JPN use salary as their assumed contribution base. Check your payslip for taxable allowances, contribution bases and December annual reconciliation.
